常见问题

InputSwitcher 如何工作？

InputSwitcher 会在您切换应用时自动切换到对应的输入法。您只需为每个应用设置一次输入法规则，之后每次切换应用时都会自动生效。

为什么需要辅助功能权限？

辅助功能权限用于监听应用切换事件和自动切换输入法。InputSwitcher 不会收集或传输任何个人数据。

开机自启功能会短暂退出，这正常吗？

是的，这是正常现象。由于 macOS 沙盒限制，启用或禁用开机自启时应用会短暂退出，这是系统行为，非 bug。

免费版和专业版有什么区别？

免费版最多支持 5 条规则。专业版一次购买永久使用，解锁无限规则、锁定功能、批量操作、导入导出、规则清理等高级功能。

如何恢复购买？

打开 InputSwitcher 菜单 → 点击「InputSwitcher Pro」→ 点击「恢复购买」按钮即可。

支持哪些语言？

当前支持简体中文、繁体中文、英文、日语、韩语、德语、法语、西班牙语、葡萄牙语（巴西）。

FAQ

How does InputSwitcher work?

InputSwitcher automatically switches your input method when you switch between applications. Just set up a rule once for each app, and it works automatically going forward.

Why does it need Accessibility permissions?

Accessibility permissions are required to monitor app switching events and automatically change input methods. InputSwitcher does not collect or transmit any personal data.

Is it normal for the app to quit briefly when toggling Launch at Login?

Yes, this is expected. Due to macOS sandbox restrictions, the app briefly quits when enabling or disabling launch at login. This is system behavior, not a bug.

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

The free version supports up to 5 rules. Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited rules, locking, batch operations, import/export, rule cleanup, and more.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the InputSwitcher menu → click "InputSwitcher Pro" → click "Restore Purchase".

Which languages are supported?

Currently supports Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish, and Brazilian Portuguese.
