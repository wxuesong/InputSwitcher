# InputSwitcher Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 22, 2026

InputSwitcher does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer or third parties.

## Data Stored on Your Mac

Application rules and preferences are stored locally using Apple system storage. InputSwitcher does not have a developer-operated server.

## iCloud Sync

InputSwitcher Pro can optionally synchronize rules and preferences through Apple's iCloud key-value store. This data is associated with your Apple ID, is handled by Apple, and is not accessible to the developer. You can disable iCloud Sync in InputSwitcher settings.

## Purchases

InputSwitcher Pro purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details. Apple may provide transaction status needed to unlock purchased features.

## Diagnostics

Debug logging is disabled by default. When enabled, switch events are stored locally on your Mac and are not uploaded by InputSwitcher.

## Contact

A public support email address must be added here before App Store submission.

# InputSwitcher 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 7 月 22 日

InputSwitcher 不会向开发者或第三方收集、传输、出售或共享个人数据。

应用规则和偏好设置保存在用户的 Mac 本地。专业版可以选择通过 Apple 的 iCloud 键值存储同步规则和偏好设置，这些数据由 Apple 处理，开发者无法访问。专业版购买由 Apple App Store 处理，开发者不会获得用户的付款信息。

调试日志默认关闭。启用后，切换记录仅保存在用户的 Mac 本地，不会由 InputSwitcher 上传。

提交 App Store 前需要在此补充公开的支持邮箱。
